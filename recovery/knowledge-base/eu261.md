# EU Regulation 261/2004 — Air Passenger Rights

Applies to flights departing from an EU airport (any airline) and flights
arriving in the EU on an EU-based carrier.

## Cancellation

When a flight is cancelled, passengers are entitled to a choice between:
- A full refund of the ticket price within 7 days, or
- Re-routing to their final destination at the earliest opportunity, or
- Re-routing at a later date at the passenger's convenience.

If the airline gives less than 14 days' notice of cancellation, passengers
may also be entitled to financial compensation unless the cancellation was
caused by extraordinary circumstances (e.g. severe weather, political
instability, security risks, air traffic control strikes).

## Compensation amounts (cancellation and long delays)

- EUR 250 for flights of 1,500 km or less.
- EUR 400 for intra-EU flights over 1,500 km and all other flights between
  1,500 km and 3,500 km.
- EUR 600 for flights over 3,500 km.

Compensation may be reduced by 50% if re-routing is offered and the arrival
time does not exceed the original scheduled arrival by more than 2–4 hours
(depending on flight distance).

## Delay

For long delays passengers are entitled to care (meals, refreshments,
communication, and accommodation if an overnight stay is required):
- 2 hours or more for flights of 1,500 km or less.
- 3 hours or more for intra-EU flights over 1,500 km and other flights
  between 1,500 and 3,500 km.
- 4 hours or more for all other flights.

If the delay at arrival is 3 hours or more, the same compensation as for
cancellation may apply, unless caused by extraordinary circumstances. If a
flight is delayed by at least 5 hours, passengers may choose a full refund
instead of travelling.
