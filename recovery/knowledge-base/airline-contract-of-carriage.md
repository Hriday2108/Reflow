# Sample Airline Contract of Carriage — Fare & Refund Clauses

These are representative sample clauses for demonstration. Replace with the
specific carrier's Contract of Carriage as the knowledge base is expanded.

## Refundable vs. non-refundable fares

- Fully refundable (flexible) fares: cancel any time before departure for a
  full refund to the original form of payment, no cancellation fee.
- Non-refundable (economy/basic) fares: not eligible for a cash refund if the
  passenger cancels. The unused fare value, minus a change or cancellation
  fee, is typically issued as a travel credit valid for 12 months from the
  original ticket issue date.
- Basic economy fares are generally non-changeable and non-refundable except
  where required by law or within the 24-hour risk-free cancellation window.

## 24-hour risk-free cancellation

Tickets purchased at least 7 days before departure may be cancelled within 24
hours of booking for a full refund with no penalty, per US DOT rule.

## Rebooking after a carrier-caused disruption

When the airline cancels or significantly delays a flight, it will rebook the
passenger on the next available flight at no additional charge, or on a
partner carrier where an interline agreement exists. If no acceptable
alternative is available, the passenger may request a full refund of the
unused portion of the ticket.

## Change fees

- Flexible fares: no change fee; fare difference may apply.
- Standard fares: change fee of USD 75–200 plus any fare difference, waived
  when the change is caused by a carrier-initiated schedule change.

## Hotel accommodation

For an overnight disruption within the airline's control, the carrier will
provide hotel accommodation and ground transportation. No accommodation is
provided for disruptions outside the airline's control (e.g. weather).
